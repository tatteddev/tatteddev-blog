I asked Codex to make a preview of every possible character in Pocket Quests. I figured I could look through them and see what was still broken. Then the number came back in the millions, and I was like, okay, what am I supposed to do with that?

I’m still having a hard time with this character creator. A big part of Pocket Quests is earning stuff for your character, and I want to be able to add things like a sword or a backpack without it turning into a whole project every time. But even with the options we have now, I can make a character that looks pretty good, change one thing, and suddenly something looks off.

There was one pose where the head looked stuck on. I kept telling Codex the neck was too skinny, or the skin was going into the collar, or whatever I could point to in that preview. It would change something, show me another version, and I’d be like, yeah, I can still see it. It turned out the head and the shoulders were drawn at different angles. I liked the painted study we did after that, but I’m still finding it hard to tell how much of that improvement carries over when I start swapping stuff out.

The eyes were easier to explain once I saw the problem. I changed the skin tone and the whites of the eyes changed too. Codex made a fix, I asked for the same face on light and dark skin, and I could still see it. At least with that one I knew what I wanted to see in the next preview. Same face, same eye color. Just change the skin.

That’s the part I keep getting stuck on. I can give Codex pretty specific feedback once I’ve seen something go wrong. I don’t always know which pose or outfit to ask it to show me in the first place. I can ask for a smaller batch, but I’m still picking what’s in it. I might check the sword on a few poses and completely miss that taking a scarf off makes the neck look weird again.

I think the creator is still pretty rough. There are combinations I know I want to look at again, and there are probably whole categories of problems I haven’t run into yet. Right now I’m still trying to figure out what to ask Codex to show me the next time I sit down with it.
